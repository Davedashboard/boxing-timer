Privacy Policy

Muay Thai Round Timer · DD-Software · Last updated 26 November 2026

Summary

Muay Thai Round Timer does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, saved presets and custom exercises are stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

  • Internet: used to load the app's fonts and to open YouTube exercise how-to searches when you tap them.
  • Vibration: used to vibrate on round changes.

The app uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech to announce exercises. This runs on your device.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and exercise links open YouTube. These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact email on the app's Google Play listing.
