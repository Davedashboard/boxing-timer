## Full description (max 4000)

```
A round timer built by someone who trains Muay Thai, for pad rounds, bag work, sparring and home conditioning.

Most timers do rounds OR workouts. This one does both.

ROUND TIMER
• Set rounds, round length and rest (up to 5 minutes each, or zero rest)
• Clapper warning 10 seconds before the end of each round
• 3-2-1 countdown beeps and a proper bell
• Get-ready countdown before you start
• Ready-made presets: Muay Thai 5 × 3:00, Boxing 3 × 3:00, Pro fight 12 rounds, bag sprints, skipping warm-up
• Save your own presets

WORKOUT BUILDER
• Build circuits from a library of exercises: boxing / Muay Thai, upper body, arms, legs, core and cardio
• Circuit or superset order, with sets and rest between sets
• Voice announces the next exercise so you don't have to look at your phone
• Quick YouTube how-to link for any exercise you're not sure of

MADE FOR TRAINING
• Big, clear display you can read from across the gym
• Keeps the screen on while the timer runs
• Vibration on round changes
• Boosted voice and loud bells, and it can lower or pause your music (Spotify etc.) while it talks
• Works offline

NO NONSENSE
• No ads
• No account or sign-up
• No tracking: your presets and exercises stay on your phone

Free, built for fun. Feedback welcome.
```

## Before uploading

Play needs a **signed release App Bundle (.aab)**. The current GitHub workflow builds a debug APK, which is fine for side-loading but Play will reject it. You need an upload key and a `bundleRelease` build first.
